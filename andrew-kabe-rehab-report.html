1. Executive Summary & Appraisal GLA Strategy

Why Conditioning All 2,086 Sq Ft & Converting the Lower Level Into a Conforming In-Law Suite Maximizes Net Proceeds

Currently, 1733 Sara Ct SE has zero active central HVAC and relies on fake wall vents in the 705 sq ft Garage Conversion. Leaving the lower level unconditioned saves a modest $2.6K–$4.5K upfront, but risks appraisers assigning near-zero contributory GLA value to those 705 sq ft—while creating buyer inspection objections when registers blow no air.

A. Half-House Central HVAC + Ductless Heat-Pump Mini-Split Install a Half-House Central Heat Pump for the Main Floor & Upstairs (~1,381 sq ft) where ductwork is straightforward, and pair it with a dedicated Ductless Heat-Pump Mini-Split (plus exterior condenser) in the 705 sq ft Garage Conversion. Every room is genuinely heated and cooled for appraisal credit without costly basement soffit ducting.
B. High-Value In-Law / Income Suite Transformation Per Magicplan Page 26 (driveway orientation), knock down the interior wall between the Front-Right driveway-entry room (106 sf, #10) and Living Room (293 sf, #12) to create an open Kitchenette, and frame a closet in the Front-Left room (139 sf, #11) so it legally markets and appraises as a true conforming bedroom.

3. Critical Pre-Listing Blind Spots to Verify

(a) Public Sewer vs. Septic Verification

Property is believed to be on public sewer, but must be verified with Rockdale Water Resources / county records before listing. In Georgia, septic tank sizing legally restricts advertised bedroom count and In-Law Suite occupancy; confirming public sewer protects marketing claims for 4 conforming bedrooms + In-Law Kitchenette.

(b) Breaker Panel, Amperage & GFCI Code

Close the open stairwell electrical breaker panel with a proper dead-front cover + labeled directory and patch cut drywall. Have a licensed electrician verify total main panel amperage capacity for the Half-House Central HVAC, Mini-Split, Kitchenette & dual laundry hookups—and upgrade all water-adjacent outlets to GFCI.

(c) Crawl Moisture & Downspout Drainage

Once falling fiberglass insulation and wood/drywall/trash debris are cleared from the 468 sf crawl space, inspect floor joists, subfloor, and black CMU walls for any water stains or moisture intrusion before listing. Eliminate the corner downspout wooden trough & purple bucket with a rigid/corrugated extension discharging water away from the foundation.
